Token Radar

Every protocol in the snapshot that records no token, ranked by expected value — what its token would be worth, times the chance it ever launches one — with a farm verdict for each.

Data: DefiLlama snapshot, 2026-11-14. A protocol "records no token" when the snapshot carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it — that is the record, not proof it has none. Expected values are rough comps estimates times a peer-record probability, not forecasts and not investment advice. Farm verdicts rest on stated assumptions (an airdrop share and a farmer count nobody has measured) — move the scenario controls and see how fragile they are. Farm at your own risk; never pay to claim an airdrop.
